Helpful Tech Tips

Quick practical tips to help you protect, maintain and maximize your devices.

Phone & Laptop Tips

• Avoid overnight charging constantly to preserve battery lifespan.
• Keep liquids away from charging ports and motherboard areas.
• Restart your device occasionally for smoother performance.
• Avoid cheap chargers that damage charging IC components.
• Always update your software for security improvements.
• Excessive heat reduces battery efficiency over time.

Gadget Buying Tips

• Check battery health before buying fairly used phones.
• Confirm original screen condition before payment.
• Always test Face ID, fingerprint and cameras.
• Avoid devices with unknown repair history.
• Compare storage variants before purchasing.
• Verify accessories quality before use.
